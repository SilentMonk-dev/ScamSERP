# Public launch gates

The local application works with budgeted live collection. A public fraud-audit service still requires the following external evidence and operational decisions.

- Independently verify official contacts for all 40 entities. The four assistant source checks are not independent human verification. Record primary source, reviewer, check date and expiry; include regional variants and corrections.
- Have native speakers validate each translated intent. Generic and specialized seeds are templates, not empirical search-demand measurements.
- Independently label at least 300 real observations, including legitimate third-party results, and measure precision, recall and official false accusations. Targets are not achieved metrics.
- Agree on sampling, coverage and freshness. Six city proxies do not establish state-wide exposure. National Autocomplete suggestions do not establish actual search volume.
- Fund and configure the collection plan. `/api/coverage` reports minimum cadence requests, actual measured cells and caps. Increase caps only after agreeing a budget; retries, enrichment and public searches add spend. The scheduler batch limit and interval must support the selected cadence.
- Confirm whether advertiser verification/account-creation evidence is available for the intended claims. The documented Transparency fields do not establish account age; unknown stays unknown. Human attribution and relationship reviews require evidence and expire.
- Review real Likely Fraud campaigns before publishing; evidence changes invalidate approval. Complete takedown/correction operations and dispute staffing.
- Operate HTTPS, restricted Maps browser keys, protected admin access, backups, monitoring and a persistent single scheduler. Test restore and process restart before public operation.
- Substantiate national-loss and novelty claims through external research before publishing them.

No automated code change can honestly complete independent human verification or create missing provider evidence.
